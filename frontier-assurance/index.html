Governance & assurance

How Frontier approaches evidence, uncertainty, reasoning and responsible evaluation — and what the research prototype has not established.

Status: experimental research | Reviewed: 17 November 2026 | Standalone public reference page. This page is descriptive, not a live audit or certification.

Research prototype — not a safety or compliance certificate.

Passing unit tests, returning source citations or generating a plausible explanation does not establish that a result is correct or that a system is safe for consequential decisions.

Current design safeguards

Design safeguard

Source-bound evidence

Reasoning uses source references, evidence identifiers and mapping records where the relevant pipeline provides them. A retrieved webpage or quoted name is not automatically a verified fact.

Design safeguard

Explicit uncertainty

The architecture distinguishes supported, opposed, conflicting and unknown states. Unsupported conclusions should remain unresolved instead of being presented as certain.

Design safeguard

Bounded inference

Some reasoning paths use limited iterations and constrained logical relationships. Temporal order alone is not evidence of causation; visual localisation is not a diagnosis.

Research control

Review before core changes

Experimental structural updates require acceptance and validation conditions. These flags are not yet a fully authenticated, independently audited approval system.

The standalone OM research engine is developed separately from Frontier. This research demonstration must not be interpreted as automatically running every change in the private OM research branch.

Uploads, privacy and external services

Before uploading:

Use synthetic, public or appropriately authorised research material. Avoid uploading identifiable people, confidential records or patient information until processing and retention arrangements are confirmed for the relevant backend.

The Frontier interface can contact remote processing services and may use browser-assisted retrieval for public web sources. Files and questions should therefore not be assumed to remain on your device. Specific data retention periods, storage locations, deletion guarantees and third-party processing terms have not been independently verified in this review.

A static GitHub Pages frontend is not equivalent to a fully local or offline system. Browser-based retrieval and backend requests have different processing paths. Users should check the processing context before submitting sensitive material.

Open assurance questions

  • Independent unseen-domain, adversarial and prompt-injection evaluations.
  • Authenticated approval records and rollback for structural core updates.
  • Documented data flow, retention, permissions and privacy impact assessment.
  • Domain-specific performance measures, human review procedures and incident management.
  • Accessibility testing of the interface, including keyboard and assistive technology checks.

These remain priorities for the next stage of evaluation. They are not represented as already completed.
