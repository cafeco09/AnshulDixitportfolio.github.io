# Portfolio strategy

## Positioning

**Decision-Centric LLM Research Engineer**

I build, post-train and evaluate language models, then engineer them into evidence-grounded, uncertainty-aware systems for complex human and organisational decisions.

## Portfolio thesis

The portfolio connects three technical layers:

1. **LLM capability engineering** — post-training, preference optimisation, model adaptation and inference efficiency.
2. **AI decision systems** — evidence synthesis, tool use, alternatives, uncertainty, recommendation and escalation.
3. **Behavioural evaluation** — constructive disagreement, appropriate reliance, calibrated communication and decision quality.

## Signature methodology: DECIDE

DECIDE is the repeatable method connecting model development to measurable decision improvement.

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **D — Define** | Define the consequential decision, stakeholders and cost of error. |
| **E — Evaluate** | Evaluate data, model performance, robustness and behavioural failure modes. |
| **C — Constrain** | Add policy, ethical, operational and commercial constraints. |
| **I — Integrate** | Integrate the model into a realistic human workflow. |
| **D — Demonstrate** | Demonstrate decision improvement through experiments and simulation. |
| **E — Establish** | Establish monitoring, accountability and continuous assurance. |

### How DECIDE applies to LLM engineering

- **Define** the decision or reasoning task the LLM must support, rather than starting with a model or fashionable technique.
- **Evaluate** the base model for capability, hallucination, calibration, sycophancy, robustness and behavioural failure modes.
- **Constrain** training and inference with evidence requirements, domain rules, risk thresholds, abstention and escalation policies.
- **Integrate** the post-trained model with retrieval, tools, structured outputs and appropriate human oversight.
- **Demonstrate** whether SFT, LoRA, DPO or system changes improve model behaviour and downstream decision quality against credible baselines.
- **Establish** regression testing, monitoring, accountability, feedback collection and continuous assurance after deployment.

DECIDE is not a governance layer added after model development. It is the operating framework for selecting, improving, integrating and validating the model.
