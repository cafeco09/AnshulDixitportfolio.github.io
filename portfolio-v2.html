DECIDE: my signature methodology

A repeatable framework for moving from a model or research idea to measurable, accountable decision improvement. DECIDE connects technical evaluation, real-world constraints, human workflow and continuous assurance.

DECIDE is not a governance checklist added after development. It is the operating method for selecting, improving, integrating and validating an AI model from the beginning.
D
Stage 01

Define

Define the consequential decision, stakeholders, desired outcome and cost of error before choosing the model or intervention.

E
Stage 02

Evaluate

Evaluate data, model performance, robustness, uncertainty and behavioural failure modes—including hallucination, sycophancy and over-reliance.

C
Stage 03

Constrain

Add policy, ethical, operational and commercial constraints, with evidence requirements, risk thresholds, abstention and escalation rules.

I
Stage 04

Integrate

Integrate the model into a realistic human workflow using retrieval, tools, structured outputs and appropriate human oversight.

D
Stage 05

Demonstrate

Demonstrate decision improvement through controlled experiments, simulation and credible comparisons against human and technical baselines.

E
Stage 06

Establish

Establish regression testing, monitoring, accountability, feedback collection and continuous assurance after deployment.

For LLM post-training, DECIDE tests whether techniques such as SFT, LoRA and DPO improve not only response quality, but also downstream reasoning, calibrated communication and human decision outcomes.
