Work in progress.

Not every idea here is finished, validated or ready for use. The Lab is where I build, test and revise approaches while keeping their limits visible.

Some work is being developed; some has a working prototype with unresolved questions; some is deliberately paused. The status notes below show what is still in progress.

Current work

What is still being built or tested.

These are active workstreams, not claims that each system is complete. Where evidence is missing or an idea has not been validated, that remains part of the description.

01 / FRONTIERWebsite integration pending

Evidence-aware reasoning architecture

Frontier is designed to distinguish evidence from inference, preserve uncertainty and return an unresolved result when the available information does not support a conclusion.

In progress: completing the Ann-On-Ally website around the frozen V3.1.1 baseline. The reasoning engine is being held steady during the website build.

Still openConnect the website experience to the prepared assistant and then evaluate it against real questions across the site.
02 / SIYAIterative development

Evidence dashboard and outreach workflow

SIYA supports bounded questions over the education dashboard. The wider concept includes authority outreach, human approval and tracking the stages of a request.

In progress: refining how questions, evidence gaps, draft outreach and review status are represented in the dashboard.

Still openImprove source coverage and definitions; validate the outreach workflow with the relevant authorities before treating it as an operational process.
03 / SCENT PROTOCOLPhysical output unresolved

Structured packets for scent devices

The protocol packages compound identity, structure, concentration and a checksum, then applies validation gates before a receiver accepts a packet.

In progress: strengthening reference coverage and the sender-to-receiver path. A test packet can support selected identity fields; that does not establish that a device can reproduce an odour.

Still openMixture handling, stereoisomer-specific coverage, independent chemical validation and reliable physical reproduction.
04 / BEHAVIOURAL AISimulation, not field validation

Explainability and psychological reactance

The Explainability Buffer simulation explores how explanations may change responses to AI advice when a recommendation conflicts with a person’s judgement.

In progress: refining the simulation questions and making the assumptions behind modelled responses explicit.

Still openIndependent replication and empirical evaluation with people are needed before drawing conclusions about real-world behaviour.
05 / PUBLIC DATACoverage and interpretation ongoing

Education, food waste and hunger dashboards

These tools bring together public indicators that are fragmented across countries, sources and reporting years, and make their definitions and gaps easier to inspect.

In progress: maintaining data provenance, clarifying measures and improving how comparisons communicate differences in coverage.

Still openSome indicators remain missing, use different periods or are modelled estimates. Comparisons can describe patterns, but do not by themselves establish causes.
06 / EXPLORATORY PHYSICSHypothesis testing

Alternative explanations for galaxy rotation

This line of work asks whether a physically constrained field could explain observed rotation curves and tests speculative ideas against astronomical data.

In progress: comparing model predictions with held-out galaxies and identifying which assumptions fail to generalise. A fixed-strength repulsive scalar model has not generalised convincingly.

Still openDevelop a falsifiable model with a clear energy source and gravitational effect; obtain the gas, magnetic-field and current-profile data needed to examine plasma-supported variants.

Open questions belong here.

A prototype is not proof, a simulation is not a user study, and a visible pattern is not automatically a causal explanation. The Lab keeps those distinctions in view while the work develops.
