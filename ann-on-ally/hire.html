How I work

Clear questions. Honest evidence.

My approach is to make the reasoning useful and inspectable, from the first question through to the outcome.

Evidence first

Separate what is observed from what is assumed.

Question before method

Start with the decision problem rather than forcing a preferred technique.

Test assumptions

Look for alternative explanations and failure modes.

Make uncertainty visible

Avoid presenting weak evidence with artificial confidence.

Build for use

Consider how the output will actually enter a decision process.
