My signature methodology: DECIDE.

DECIDE is the operating method connecting model development with consequential decisions. It begins before training and continues after deployment, so technical performance, behavioural effects and organisational accountability are designed together.

It is not a governance checklist added after the model is built. It is a repeatable framework for selecting, enhancing, integrating and validating AI systems from the beginning.

D

Define

Define the consequential decision, stakeholders, available actions and cost of error.

E

Evaluate

Evaluate data, model performance, robustness, uncertainty and behavioural failure modes.

C

Constrain

Add policy, ethical, operational and commercial constraints, including abstention and escalation.

I

Integrate

Integrate the model with evidence, tools, structured outputs and a realistic human workflow.

D

Demonstrate

Demonstrate decision improvement through experiments, simulation and credible baselines.

E

Establish

Establish monitoring, regression tests, accountability and continuous assurance.
